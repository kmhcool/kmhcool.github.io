About · 02

Curious by default.
Practical on purpose.

I’m Thanawat, also known online as kmhcool. I like projects that touch real life: buses that arrive somewhere, exits people can find, logs you can verify, and services that keep running on imperfect hardware.

My process usually starts with a concrete inconvenience. I trace the system around it, build the smallest useful version, then keep testing until the software behaves sensibly outside the happy path.
